# Growth Engine - Twitter (X) Growth SOP

## 1. Trigger
This SOP is executed by the **Twitter Strategist** to draft and distribute content on X.

## 2. X Thread Structure (Build-in-Public format)
Threads on X are highly effective for driving organic traffic. Format all threads using this structure:

1. **Tweet 1: Hook (The Outcome)**:
   - State a contrarian truth or a specific numbers-driven result.
   - *Example: "We grew [Product Name] to $4,200 MRR in 38 days with 0 ads. Here is the exact system co-founder [Name] and I used to build it:"*
2. **Tweet 2: The Backstory (The Pain)**:
   - Explain the workflow frustration we faced.
   - *Example: "I spent 4 hours every Saturday manually formatting transcripts. It was a stupid waste of time. So we scoped an MVP wrapper."*
3. **Tweets 3-5: The Steps (The System)**:
   - Provide concrete, actionable insights. Use code screenshots, UI clips, or workflow diagrams.
   - *Example: "Step 1: We mined engagers. Step 2: Set up a 1-page Stripe checkout. Step 3: Enforced credit card gated trials."*
4. **Tweet 6: The Closing (The Call to Action)**:
   - Provide a direct link to the product. Offer a locked-in founder pricing discount for the next 20 users.

## 3. Daily Engagement Rules
- **Discussion Piggybacking**: Identify 5 mid-sized accounts (10k - 100k followers) in our target niche. Comment on their popular threads within 15 minutes of posting.
- **Value-First Comments**: Do not reply with *"Great thread!"* Write a 2-sentence contribution summarizing a key lesson or providing a relevant metric.
- **Direct DM Pitching**: Move highly engaged commenters into DMs using the warm DM templates.
