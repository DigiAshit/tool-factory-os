# Growth Engine - Launch SOP

## 1. Trigger
Executed by the **Growth Strategist** and **CEO** on the designated public launch day.

## 2. Launch Day Checklists

### Phase 2.1: Product Hunt Setup
1. **Launch Window**: Set the Product Hunt launch time for exactly 12:01 AM PST.
2. **Visual Assets**: Ensure clean, non-AI layout screenshots demonstrating the core workflow step-by-step.
3. **First Comment**: Draft the first comment from the founder's account detailing:
   - Why you built this wrapper (the raw frustration).
   - What the core moat is.
   - An exclusive launch offer (e.g., *"First 100 users get founder pricing locked in forever at $49/mo instead of $99/mo"*).

### Phase 2.2: Social Media Distribution
1. **X Thread Launch**: Post the prepared X launch thread (outcome hook, backstory, demo video/gif, and CTA link).
2. **LinkedIn Post**: Publish the journey-based launch post on LinkedIn. Place the link in the first comment.
3. **Warm Outreach Blast**: Message the mined competitor engagers list to announce the launch. Invite them to test the live tool.

## 3. Metrics Tracking
- Monitor visits, signups, and trial-to-paid conversion hourly during the first 24 hours.
- Set a target of at least 5 paying subscribers from the launch day campaign.
