# Growth Engine - Growth SOP

## 1. Trigger
Executed by the **Growth Strategist** to scale traffic and implement compounding distribution loops.

## 2. The 60-Day Concurrent Channel Test
- **The Heuristic**: Do not try to run 5 marketing channels indefinitely. It dilutes focus and energy.
- **The Execution**:
  - Select **three acquisition channels** to test concurrently for 60 days (e.g., X Threads, LinkedIn brand building, and Cold Email/Warm DMs).
  - Track customer acquisition cost (CAC), lead volume, and conversion rate for each channel.
  - **At Day 60**: Run a review gate. Kill the two worst-performing channels immediately. Double down 100% of growth energy and resources on the single winning channel.

## 3. Compounding Distribution Loops

### Section 3.1: Affiliate Program Setup (Rewardful)
- **Tooling**: Integrate **Rewardful** with Stripe to manage tracking automatically.
- **Offer Structure**: Offer a highly competitive **30% recurring lifetime commission** to affiliates.
- **Affiliate Target**: Reach out to existing power users and influencers who already serve our creator/digital entrepreneur audience.

### Section 3.2: Built-in Product Viral Loops
- **Watermarks**: Place a subtle, beautiful product watermark (e.g., *"Generated by [Product Name]"*) on free/trial outputs.
- **Share triggers**: Provide a 1-click Twitter share button immediately after a user completes a successful core workflow.
