# Growth Engine — Growth (Channels & Loops)

## 2. The 60-day concurrent channel test
- Don't run 5 channels forever — it dilutes focus. Select **exactly 3** to test
  concurrently for 60 days (e.g. X threads, LinkedIn brand building, cold email/warm DMs).
- Track CAC, lead volume, and conversion per channel in `channel_scorecard`. Record CAC
  as **both first-touch and last-touch** (see metrics.yaml#definitions.cac).
- **Day 60 gate:** before killing the two "worst," check assist contribution — X content
  often feeds DMs; LinkedIn feeds warm intros. Do not kill a channel on last-touch CAC
  alone if it assists conversions. Then concentrate 100% of energy on the winner.
- Cold email channel must satisfy `_shared/metrics.yaml#platform_guardrails.cold_email`
  (unsubscribe + suppression list).

## 3. Compounding distribution loops
### 3.1 Affiliate (Rewardful + Stripe)
- Integrate Rewardful with Stripe for automatic tracking.
- Offer up to **30% recurring**, capped at
  `affiliate_commission_max_months` (12 months — not "lifetime"; lifetime commissions
  compound into a permanent margin liability).
- Recruit existing power users / influencers already serving the audience.

### 3.2 Built-in viral loops
- Subtle product watermark on free/trial outputs ("Generated by {Product}").
- 1-click share button immediately after a successful core-workflow completion.

## Exit
Complete on all `done_when` conditions.
