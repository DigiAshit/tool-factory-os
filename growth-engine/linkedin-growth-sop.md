# Growth Engine - LinkedIn Growth SOP

## 1. Trigger
This SOP is executed daily by the **LinkedIn Strategist** and the **Founder** to build organic distribution.

## 2. Daily Posting Strategy: Journey-Based Updates
- **Philosophy**: Do not post generic business advice or corporate press releases. People connect with individuals building in public. Document the raw founder journey.
- **Topics**: Focus on real data, micro-lessons, and transparency:
  - **Revenue Updates**: Share recent MRR updates (both gains and losses) with context on what worked and what failed.
  - **Behind-the-Scenes Building**: Show screenshots of raw UI iterations, codebase simplifications, or co-founder discussions.
  - **Feature Pauses/Kills**: Share why you killed a feature or paused a product. Detail the lesson learned.
  - **Operational Insights**: Post about E-myth automation, systems management, and holding company structures.

## 3. Post Formats and Heuristics
1. **Hook**: Start with a single, high-impact line containing a specific metric or concrete outcome.
   - *Example: "We spent $1,200 on API costs last month. Here is exactly what we learned about scaling AI wrappers."*
2. **Body**: Short, punchy sentences (maximum 2 lines per paragraph) outlining the problem, the test, the result, and the rule.
3. **Closing**: End with an interactive question to spark conversation (e.g., *"How do you handle API cost scalability? Reply below."*).
4. **Frequency**: Post exactly once per day, ideally in the morning (between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM local time).
5. **No External Links in Post**: Always place outbound links (to product landing pages or newsletters) in the first comment to avoid algorithmic distribution penalties.
