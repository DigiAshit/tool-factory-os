# Growth Engine - Distribution SOP

## 1. Trigger
Executed daily by the **Twitter Strategist** and **LinkedIn Strategist** to tap into existing attention streams.

## 2. Conversation Piggybacking Playbook

### Step 2.1: Monitor Target Accounts
1. **Curate Lists**: Maintain a list of 10-15 industry influencers and mid-sized accounts (10k - 100k followers) who actively speak to our target audience (creators, builders, marketers).
2. **Notification Triggers**: Turn on mobile alerts or RSS notifications for posts from these target accounts.

### Step 2.2: The 15-Minute Response Rule
1. **Speed is Key**: When a target account posts a new thread, respond within **15 minutes** to secure high placement in the replies.
2. **Constructive Contribution**:
   - Write a 2-3 sentence comment that adds concrete value, shares a data point, or clarifies a common misconception.
   - Do not promote the product in the primary response.
   - *Example: "We noticed this exact bottleneck. Our API logs showed that 82% of latency occurs at the formatting step. Gating the script saved us 4 hours."*
3. **Soft Pitch (Sub-reply)**: If your primary comment gets high engagement (5+ likes), reply to your own comment with a soft link to the product:
   - *"We automated this step into a tiny script. If anyone is stuck here, try it out: [URL]"*
