# Growth Engine - Founder Authority SOP

## 1. Trigger
This SOP is executed by the **LinkedIn Strategist** and **Twitter Strategist** to define the positioning guidelines for all public distribution channels.

## 2. Positioning & Credibility Rules

### Rule 2.1: The Rule of Real Numbers
- **No Vague Claims**: Avoid terms like *"massive growth,"* *"huge volume,"* or *"thousands of users."* Instead, use precise numbers sourced from database scorecards or Stripe dashboards:
  - *Incorrect: "Our video tool is growing fast!"*
  - *Correct: "We grew from 12 WAU to 87 WAU in 14 days by changing the CTA text."*
- **Financial Transparency**: Share Stripe screenshot clips showing net revenue, growth curves, or transaction logs to provide concrete proof.

### Rule 2.2: The "Different vs Better" Framework
- **Positioning over Features**: Do not argue that your software is "better" than a giant competitor by listing 5 minor features. Position it as **fundamentally different** for a specific sub-audience:
  - *Competitor Positioning: "We are an all-in-one content platform with 200 settings."*
  - *Our Positioning: "We are a text-to-video wrapper for creators who need to generate 1 Reel in under 60 seconds with 1 click."*
- **Language of the Frustrated User**: Use the exact phrases recorded in the Opportunity Engine discovery log. Reflecting user frustration builds instant authority because the customer feels understood.

## 3. Quality Check
- Audit all drafts before publishing. Ensure that every claim has a supporting number, visual screenshot, or specific user quote.
