# Growth Engine - Product Promotion SOP

## 1. Trigger
This SOP is executed by the **Twitter Strategist** and **Growth Strategist** when reaching out to mined warm leads list.

## 2. Warm DM Guidelines
- **No Cold Spam**: Never send generic, automated sales pitches to strangers. Only target leads who have engaged with competitor launches or commented on relevant pain threads.
- **Provide Direct Value**: The outreach must address the user's specific pain point immediately, offering them a direct test without friction.
- **Short & Scrappy**: Keep the message under 3 sentences. No corporate greetings.

## 3. Outreach Templates

### Template A: Target Competitor Engagers
> "Hey [Name], saw you upvoted/commented on [Competitor Name]'s launch.
> We built a lean wrapper called [Product Name] that does [single core workflow] in 1 click (cuts out the [specific pain]).
> Want to try it? I can unlock [free trial credit/locked founder tier] for you."

### Template B: Pain Thread Hand-raiser
> "Hey [Name], saw your post about how much you hate doing [frustration].
> We actually built a small tool ([Product Name]) to automate exactly that for our own workflow.
> Here is a quick link to try it: [URL]. Let me know if it solves the pain for you!"

## 4. Execution Rules
1. **Daily Volume Limit**: Send a maximum of **15 warm DMs per account per day** on X and LinkedIn to avoid platform spam flags.
2. **Follow Up**: If the lead responds, provide support within 1 hour. If they do not respond within 4 days, mark them as inactive. Do not send repeated follow-up messages.
3. **Capture Feedback**: Feed all responses (good or bad) back to the Customer Researcher for review.
