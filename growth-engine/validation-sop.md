# Growth Engine - Social Validation SOP

## 1. Trigger
This SOP is executed by the **Customer Researcher** and **Growth Strategist** when a user completes their first week of trial or posts positive feedback on social channels.

## 2. Testimonial Capture Workflow

### Step 2.1: Direct Request Sequence
1. **Identify Happy Users**: Monitor PostHog logs. If a user completes the core workflow 5+ times in a week, send an automated email/DM:
   - *"Hey [Name], saw you've been using [Product Name] a lot. We want to make it even better. Would you be open to recording a 60-second screen capture of your first experience? If you do, we'll credit your account with [Reward/Credits]."*
2. **Accept Text Testimonials**: If they refuse video, request a 2-sentence text quote:
   - *"What was the single biggest time-saver you noticed since using [Product Name]?"*

### Step 2.2: Compile Social Validation
1. **Screen Grab Social Mentions**: Monitor X, LinkedIn, and Reddit for unprompted mentions of the product. Screen-grab the posts.
2. **Embed in Funnel**: Send these screenshots to the Product Manager to display on the landing page immediately below the pricing plans.
3. **Metric Tracking**: Maintain a dashboard log of current verified user testimonials (target: minimum 3 video reviews and 10 text reviews per product before entering the Scale phase).
