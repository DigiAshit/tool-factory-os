# Growth Engine - Content Repurposing SOP

## 1. Trigger
Executed by the **Content Strategist** weekly to maximize distribution footprint of recorded media assets.

## 2. Short Video Repurposing Workflow
When the founder records a podcast episode, a product demo, or a build-in-public update, repurpose it into short-form vertical video assets:

### Step 2.1: Extract Micro-Assets
1. Identify high-impact hooks containing a key metric or contrarian statement (e.g., first 30 seconds of an explanation).
2. Cut the video into 30-60 second vertical clips (9:16 aspect ratio).
3. Add clean, large typography captions. Avoid generic styling.

### Step 2.2: Cross-Posting Checklist
Post the vertical clips concurrently across all five platforms:
- **TikTok**: Optimize description with 3-4 target niche tags.
- **YouTube Shorts**: Post with a keyword-optimized title.
- **Instagram Reels**: Add a short description. Place the product CTA in the bio link.
- **LinkedIn**: Upload natively as a video post. Place the CTA link in the first comment.
- **X (Twitter)**: Post as an native video tweet. Add a 1-sentence hook and CTA.

## 3. Metrics
- Track video views and click-through rates (CTR) to product links.
- Focus on producing 2 high-quality vertical clips per week.
