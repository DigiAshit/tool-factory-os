# Retention Framework - Growth Engine

This document defines rules for maintaining high customer retention and driving organic traffic.

## 1. The Churn Heuristic
- Churn is the ultimate indicator of product-market fit.
- If churn exceeds **8% per month**, freeze all growth marketing and acquisition spending. Double down on simplifying the core skeleton and interviewing users who canceled.

## 2. Retention vs. Vanity Features
- Ignore feature requests that are just aesthetic preferences or edge cases.
- Prioritize features that:
  - Save the user clicks or seconds on their core workflow.
  - Automatically bring the user back (e.g. notifications, summaries).
  - Solve data backup/export friction.

## 3. SEO Moat: The Human-AI Content Loop
To build organic search traffic without triggering Google search spam penalties:
1. **Drafting**: Use AI (with templates and target keywords) to write a comprehensive, long-tail draft.
2. **Polishing**: A human operator reads the draft and rewrites sections to ensure:
   - Practical utility (no generic introductory fillers like "In today's digital landscape...").
   - Clear tone, active voice, and formatting.
   - Elimination of repetitive phrases and artificial transitions.
3. **Schedule**: Publish **1 article per day** to establish a natural human cadence. This combination of speed (AI) and quality (human) creates a highly stable SEO moat.
