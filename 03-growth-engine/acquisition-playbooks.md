# Acquisition Playbooks - Growth Engine

This document contains actionable playbooks for user acquisition during the Broadcast and Amplification phases.

## 1. Social Broadcasting (Month 3-4)
People do not follow products; they follow journeys.
- **Rule**: Post daily on X/LinkedIn showing the building journey.
- **Content**: Share transparent metrics (revenue, visitors, server costs), failures, feedback snippets, and technical decisions.
- **Heuristic**: Do not write polished marketing copy. Write like a human builder sharing progress with peers.

## 2. Direct Warm DMs (10x/Day)
Target 10 personalized reach-outs per day using these three templates. Modify them to fit the exact context.

### Template 1: Problem-Based (Warm Context)
Use when they have commented or posted about a specific problem:
> "Hey [Name], saw your post about [specific problem]. Built [product] for exactly this - [one sentence on what it does]. Worth 10 mins to show you?"

### Template 2: Audience-Based (Feedback Request)
Use when targeting an influencer or authority who represents your audience:
> "Hey [Name], building [product] for [their exact audience type]. Noticed you work with them too. Would love your take on whether this solves a real problem for them. 10 min call?"

### Template 3: Competitor Switch
Use when they are active users of a competitor:
> "Hey [Name], saw you're using [competitor]. Building something similar but [key positioning difference]. Would you be open to trying it? Happy to give you free access in exchange for feedback."

## 3. The 60-Day Channel Test
When you reach the Amplification Phase ($1k-$5k MRR), launch three channels concurrently for 60 days:
1. **SEO**: Build long-tail content (using the Human-AI loop).
2. **Ads**: Set a small test budget ($300-500/mo) and track CAC vs LTV.
3. **Affiliates**: Offer 30% recurring commission and provide ready-made promotional graphics and templates.

**The Focus Rule**: At the end of 60 days, evaluate metrics. **Pick the single channel that performs best and kill the other two.** Focus beats diversification.
