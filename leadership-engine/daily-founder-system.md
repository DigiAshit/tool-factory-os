# Leadership Engine - Daily Founder System

## 1. Trigger
Executed daily by the **CEO** and **Portfolio Manager** to manage their time across multiple products.

## 2. The Daily Schedule Architecture

### Section 2.1: Block Scheduling
- **Coordination Block (1 Hour - Morning)**:
  - Check scorecard updates.
  - Review Slack/Discord messages from co-founder makers.
  - Clear out operational blockers (e.g., approving API limits, reviewing a spec).
  - Do not write any code or draft posts during this block.
- **Deep Work Block (3 Hours - Mid-day)**:
  - Focus entirely on distribution execution (writing LinkedIn posts, X threads, or outreach DMs).
  - Turn off all notifications. Avoid multi-tasking or email checking.
- **Review Block (1 Hour - Afternoon)**:
  - Review user recordings (PostHog session replays).
  - Gather and categorize JTBD feedback.

### Section 2.2: Energy & Stamina Rules
- **Limit Context Switching**: Never attempt to build and market concurrently. Dedicate specific blocks to specific actions.
- **No Salaries Rule**: Reinforce the bootstrapped mentality. Keeping personal expenses low keeps the holding company runway infinite.
- **Apprentice Leverage**: Recruit junior operators or apprentices to run repetitive tasks (e.g., lead data scraping, short video editing) once they are standardized into SOPs.
