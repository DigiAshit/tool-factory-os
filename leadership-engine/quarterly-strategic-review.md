# Leadership Engine - Quarterly Strategic Review SOP

## 1. Trigger
Executed by the **CEO** at the end of each fiscal quarter.

## 2. Quarterly Profit Audits & Reserves
- **Holding Company Reserves**: Audit the TMaker bank accounts. Ensure that 20% of net profits across the portfolio are held in a central reserve to fund hosting, API costs, and future MVP validation campaigns.
- **Profit Distribution Reconciliation**: Audit all monthly co-founder payouts. Verify that splits match the project agreements exactly.

## 3. Annual Co-Founder Face-to-Face Retreat
To maintain alignment and build trust across a global, remote-first team:
1. **Cadence**: Organize one in-person coworking week per year.
2. **Attendees**: All co-founder builders and the CEO.
3. **Agenda**:
   - Work from the same physical workspace for 5 consecutive days.
   - Refactor critical shared code structures.
   - Align on the 12-month portfolio growth strategy.
   - Re-evaluate the $100k MRR Spin-off Gates.
4. **Funding**: TMaker holding company budgets 100% of the travel and lodging costs from central reserves.
