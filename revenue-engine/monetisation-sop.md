# Revenue Engine - Monetisation SOP

## 1. Trigger
This SOP is reviewed weekly by the **Revenue Strategist** and **CEO** to audit active project financials.

## 2. Core Billing Metrics & Funnels
Track the following metrics inside Stripe:
1. **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Run monthly revenue audits.
2. **LTV/CAC Ratio**: LTV must exceed CAC by a **minimum factor of 3** (LTV/CAC > 3).
3. **Weekly Active Cohort Churn**: Track the percentage of users in each weekly signup cohort who cancel.

## 3. The 8% Cohort Churn Gate
- **The Threshold**: If the cohort churn rate for any active product exceeds **8%**, it represents a critical issue with product quality or customer onboarding.
- **The Action**:
  - **Freeze Marketing**: Immediately halt all paid acquisition campaigns, social launches, and growth outreach.
  - **Redirect Focus**: Shift 100% of the developer and PM focus back to fixing product bugs, optimizing the core interface, and analyzing PostHog user recordings.
  - Do not resume marketing until cohort churn falls below 8%.
