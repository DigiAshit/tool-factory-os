# Revenue Engine - Umbrella Company Equity & Partner Allocation SOP

## 1. Trigger
Executed by the **CEO** and **SOP Manager** when recruiting builder co-founders and drafting project agreement terms.

## 2. The Holding Company Umbrella Architecture (TMaker Model)
We do not build separate legal entities, register multiple LLCs, or open multiple banking accounts for early-stage products. All assets are managed under a single corporate umbrella (**TMaker**).
- **Centralized Finance**: TMaker pays all project direct expenses (hosting, AI API fees, tooling) and receives 100% of product revenues.
- **Clean Books**: Maintain separate sub-ledgers in Stripe and Xero to track revenue and expenses for each specific product.

## 3. Co-Founder Equity & Profit Share Heuristics
- **1 Co-Founder per Project**: Partner with one builder co-founder per asset. Stay small to remain fast and keep decision loops short.
- **Equity Splits**: Split project ownership (usually 50-50 for equivalent contribution). 
- **No Salaries**: To ensure maximum commitment and focus, co-founders and founders receive **zero salary**.
- **Monthly profit distribution formula**: At the end of each month, calculate net cash profit for each product:
  $$\text{Net Profit} = \text{Product Revenue} - \text{Direct Product Expenses (Hosting/API/Stripe fees)}$$
  Distribute profit shares directly to the co-founders' accounts based on their ownership percentage.

## 4. The $100k MRR Legal Spin-off Gate
- **Lean Phase (<$100k MRR)**: The project remains hosted entirely inside the TMaker holding structure. Avoid wasting legal/administrative capital.
- **Split Clause**: Place a mandatory clause in every co-founder agreement:
  - If the product hits **$100k Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** and maintains it for 2 consecutive months, any co-founder can enforce the creation of a dedicated legal entity.
  - Upon creation of the new dedicated company, all product intellectual property (IP), code, and customer databases are transferred cleanly from TMaker to the new entity.
