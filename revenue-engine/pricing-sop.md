# Revenue Engine - Pricing SOP

## 1. Trigger
This SOP is executed by the **Revenue Strategist** and **CEO** when deciding the initial pricing and subscription models for approved products.

## 2. Core Heuristics

### Heuristic 2.1: The $40 - $100 B2B Pricing Band
- **Target Audience Alignment**: We build specifically for prosumers, creators, and business users. They are willing to pay for software that saves time or generates revenue.
- **The Band**: Price B2B products strictly within the **$40 to $100 per month** range.
  - Do not price below $40/mo (attracts low-budget users who flood support with tickets and churn instantly).
  - Do not price above $100/mo initially (requires high-touch sales, contract reviews, and custom demos, which goes against our E-myth systems model).
- **Simple Packages**: Keep pricing stupid simple. Use a single flat pricing structure (e.g., $99/mo for flat usage limit) or Good-Better-Best (GBB) pricing with clear usage metrics.

### Heuristic 2.2: Credit Card Gated Free Trials
- **Requirement**: Almost every product we build must require a credit card before a user can start their trial.
- **Why this works**:
  - Requires commitment from users, filtering out casual browsers who consume API resources and generate support volume.
  - Focuses co-founders on supporting serious customers.
  - Yields higher-quality feedback and higher trial-to-paid conversion rates.
- **Exceptions**: viral apps with zero marginal API/server cost (though most TFOS projects contain AI wrapper API limits, making the credit card gate mandatory).
