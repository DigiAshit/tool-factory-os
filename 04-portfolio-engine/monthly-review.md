# Monthly Review - Portfolio Engine

This document defines the agenda and checklist for the Monthly Portfolio Review, conducted by the CEO and equity partners.

## 1. Meeting Agenda
1. **Financial Overview**: Calculate total portfolio MRR, gross margins, and growth rate.
2. **Asset Performance Audit**: Group every active product in the portfolio into one of four actions:
   - **Keep**: High retention, low maintenance. (Maintain existing setup).
   - **Improve**: Moderate retention with clear feedback on core friction. (Assign product manager and coder to fix friction).
   - **Scale**: Strong validation and user activity. (Allocate additional budget to the working growth channel).
   - **Kill**: Stagnant revenue, low retention, or missed validation goals. (Initiate sunset protocol).

## 2. Resource Allocation Rule
- Always shift resources (development time, marketing budget) from products classified as "Improve" or "Kill" to the product classified as "Scale".
- Never fund a new MVP if there is an active product in the "Scale" phase that requires attention.
- Limit: Maximum of **1 active MVP** in development at any time.
