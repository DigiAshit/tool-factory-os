# Scale Framework - Portfolio Engine

This document outlines the processes for doubling down on a validated growth channel once it is proven to be the primary acquisition driver.

## 1. Scale Path: SEO Double-Down
If SEO is selected as the primary growth channel:
- **Hire a Dedicated Writer**: Bring on a writer to execute the Human-AI Content Loop.
- **Daily Cadence**: Publish exactly 1 high-quality, polished post per day.
- **Backlink Moat**: Run standard guest post outreach. Avoid link farms. Target a Domain Rating (DR) improvement of +3 per quarter.

## 2. Scale Path: Affiliate Program
If affiliates are the primary channel:
- **Commission Incentives**: Increase commission to **40% recurring** for top promoters.
- **Frictionless Onboarding**: Provide a shared asset folder containing pre-designed banners, promotional email copy, social templates, and specific feature talking points.
- **Tiered Partners**: Invite top affiliates to partner meetings. Offer them equity or beta access to future portfolio products in exchange for distribution.

## 3. Scale Path: Social Distribution
If socials are the primary channel:
- **Founder Brand**: Increase the posting frequency of the founder's account to 2x per day.
- **Creator Equity Program**: Offer micro-equity (e.g. 0.1% to 0.5% in the specific asset) to key creators in exchange for monthly promotion.
- **Own the Category**: Build a dedicated newsletter (using Feather.so or similar) that compiles industry lessons, establishing authority independent of the specific SaaS product.
