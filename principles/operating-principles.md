# Tool Factory OS - Operating Principles

## 1. Systems Over Results
- **Focus on Consistency**: In the spirit of *The E-Myth Revisited*, our business operates as a franchise prototype. We focus on the consistency and repeatability of our systems, rather than obsessing daily over unpredictable outcomes.
- **Repeatable SOPs**: If our system dictates launching 1 MVP per month or sending 10 warm outreaches per day, we execute the process meticulously. The results are a natural byproduct of running the system.

## 2. Federer's 54% Rule (Fail Fast)
- **Accept Failure as Part of the Game**: In professional tennis, even the absolute best players (like Roger Federer) only win about 54% of the individual points they play. Yet, they win tournaments because they accept the 46% loss rate as a cost of doing business.
- **Rapid Reset**: If a project fails to validate or fails its Month 2 skeleton check, we kill it immediately without hesitation. We reset, analyze our learnings, and commit to the next opportunity in our portfolio queue.

## 3. The Single-Umbrella Operating Framework
- **TMaker Centralization**: All projects route their revenues and expenses through TMaker's central accounts. This eliminates the legal costs, administrative complexity, and mental distraction of managing multiple entities.
- **Contractual Clarity**: Every co-founder relationship is governed by a standard TMaker Co-Founder Agreement prior to shipping any code.
  - **Equity Split**: Custom equity splits based on project contribution (typically 50-50 for equal maker/distributor effort).
  - **No Salaries**: Absolute focus on profit sharing to maximize alignment and commitment.
  - **IP Assignment**: All code and IP created belong to TMaker until a separate spin-off entity is created.
  - **The Split Clause**: Upon hitting $100k MRR, the project may be spun off into a separate dedicated corporation with IP transferred cleanly.
- **Annual Face-to-Face**: Co-founders must meet in person at least once a year. Sharing a workspace for a week builds trust that remote text interactions cannot match.
