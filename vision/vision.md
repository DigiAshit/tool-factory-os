# Tool Factory OS - Vision Statement

## 1. Bootstrapped, Profit-First Core
We do not build software to chase venture capital valuations, nor do we build empty demos. We build highly efficient, bootstrapped, profit-first software assets. 
- **Profitability is a Requirement**: Every project must generate revenue from day one. If a project cannot reach monetization, it represents a failure in validation, not an invitation to build more features.
- **Volume Creates Luck**: We launch multiple attempts rapidly. We accept that many attempts will fail (the 54% rule). Rather than over-engineering a single product for months, we run rapid cycles to discover what truly resonates with our audience.

## 2. The Holding Company Umbrella Brand
All software products we build are launched under a single parent holding company (**TMaker**).
- **Lean Legal Setup**: We do not create separate corporate entities or open new bank accounts for early-stage MVPs. Every project is hosted, funded, and managed by TMaker.
- **Profit Distributions**: We pay no founder or co-founder salaries. All co-founders split equity in the product. At the end of each month, TMaker calculates the net profit for each project and distributes profit shares to the co-founders based on their ownership percentages.
- **The $100k MRR Gate**: We only split a project into a dedicated legal entity once it reaches $100k Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR). At that milestone, co-founders can enforce the split, transferring all intellectual property cleanly to the new entity.

## 3. High-Leverage Co-Founders
Our organizational structure is built on 2-person dream teams:
- **1 Builder Co-Founder per Project**: The co-maker's primary responsibility is product execution (writing clean code, maintaining stability).
- **The Distribution/Ideation Lead**: The founder's primary responsibility is distribution, marketing, and sourcing high-probability opportunities.
- **High Trust & Global Collaboration**: We work with talented co-founders globally. To reinforce alignment and build long-term trust, co-founders meet in person at least once a year.
