# Opportunity Engine - Validation SOP

## 1. Trigger
This SOP is executed by the **Customer Researcher** and **Growth Strategist** immediately after logging a high-scoring opportunity and before writing any product code.

## 2. The Week 1 Validation Check

### Step 2.1: Social & Direct Outreach
1. **Pitch the Problem (No Product)**: Draft an outreach post or send direct messages to the target leads list (mined from competitor launches). Explain the specific frustration and ask:
   - *"I'm working on a script to automate [frustration]. Does anyone else experience this?"*
   - *"If I build a small tool that does [single core workflow] in under 5 minutes, would you want to test it?"*
2. **Post Formats**: Use simple, text-only posts on X or LinkedIn detailing your own personal frustration with the workflow. Do not pitch a company name or a landing page. Keep it organic and raw.

### Step 2.2: The 10+ Unprompted Leads Gate
1. **Track Inbound Interest**: Monitor post comments, replies, and DMs for 7 days.
2. **Define a Lead**: A lead is a target user who actively replies with interest, asks for access, or says *"I need this."*
3. **The Kill Decision**:
   - **PASS**: If you receive **10 or more unprompted responses** from target users wanting to test the solution within 7 days, proceed to scoring and scoping.
   - **FAIL**: If you receive **fewer than 10 responses**, kill the project immediately. Archive the idea, reset, and move to the next item in the opportunity pipeline.

## 3. Quality Check
- Verify that no code has been written prior to hitting the 10+ unprompted leads gate.
- Maintain a verification log of the 10+ leads (names, URLs, and specific messages of interest).
