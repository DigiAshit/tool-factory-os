# Opportunity Engine - Discovery SOP

## 1. Trigger
This SOP is executed weekly by the **Opportunity Researcher** to continuously surface high-probability customer frustrations and leads.

## 2. Process Steps

### Step 2.1: Social Listening & Pain Mapping
1. **Target Platforms**: Reddit, HackerNews, X, and specialized niche forums where creators and digital entrepreneurs hang out.
2. **Keyword Searches**: Focus search queries on frustration-indicative phrases:
   - *"Is there a tool to..."*
   - *"Why does it take so long to..."*
   - *"Is there an alternative to..."*
   - *"I hate doing [X] every day"*
   - *"I would pay $50/mo if someone built..."*
3. **Log Frustrations**: Extract the raw language used by customers to describe their frustrations. Do not translate their words into feature speak. Keep the exact user language (essential for copy and product positioning later).

### Step 2.2: Competitor Launch Lead Mining
1. **Target Product Launches**: Monitor Product Hunt, X, and indie builder launch announcements.
2. **Identify Mid-Sized Competitors**: Identify direct competitors in the $1k - $20k MRR range solving similar workflow problems.
3. **Mine Engagers**: Export public lists of engagers (reposters, commenters, upvoters) from competitor launch threads or pages.
4. **Compile Lead Lists**: Import these engagers into a central lead tracker. These represent high-intent "warm leads" who are actively looking for solutions to the core problem.

## 3. Quality Check
- Verify that a minimum of 5 distinct workflow frustrations have been logged each week.
- Verify that at least 50 target engagers have been extracted and logged for lead campaigns.
