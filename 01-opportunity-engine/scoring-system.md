# Scoring System - Opportunity Engine

This scoring rubric is used to quantitatively grade and approve opportunities before they enter the Product Engine.

## 1. Scoring Rubric

Every opportunity is scored from 1 to 10 across six categories:

| Category | Description | 1-3 (Low) | 4-7 (Medium) | 8-10 (High) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Demand** | Stated frustrations and lead feedback volume. | Low noise, no unprompted DMs. | Moderate social buzz; some search volume. | 10+ unprompted DMs; high keyword search intent. |
| **Monetisation** | Willingness to pay. | Users expect it for free/cheap tool. | Standard SaaS pricing ($10-30/mo) acceptable. | High business ROI (e.g. saves hours for paid agencies). |
| **Competition** | Market crowding and positioning clarity. | Giants dominate with identical positioning. | Existing tools but clear space for custom positioning. | No direct competitors in the specific niche; market validated. |
| **Build Complexity** | Execution time and technical risk. | Needs complex AI models, high infra cost, >30 days. | Standard tech stack; minor integrations. | 1-2 core workflows; can build skeleton in 1-2 weeks. |
| **Retention Potential** | Daily/weekly workflow integration. | One-time utility tool (high churn). | Monthly utility (billing, occasional audit). | Daily workflow integration (content scheduling, daily reports). |
| **Expansion Potential** | Growth into adjacent workflows. | Single-feature utility with no natural extensions. | Can add 1-2 related features for same user. | Large room to cross-sell to same user group. |

## 2. Calculation & Target Threshold
- **Total Possible Score**: 60
- **Threshold**: **40+** is required for an opportunity to be approved for build.
- **Reject Decision**: Any score below 40 is immediately killed. No exceptions.
