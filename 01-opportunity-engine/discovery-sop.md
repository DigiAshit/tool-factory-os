# Discovery SOP - Opportunity Engine

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the system for discovering and evaluating software workflow opportunities.

## 1. Objective
Consistently identify manual, repetitive, or frustrating workflows experienced by SEO professionals and digital marketing agencies, and build a list of warm leads.

## 2. Channels for Active Listening
Scan the following channels daily for organic frustrations:
- **Reddit**: Subreddits like `r/seo`, `r/marketing`, `r/indiehackers`, `r/webdev`. Look for threads asking "How do I do X automatically?" or complaining "Tool Y is too expensive/complex."
- **Forums & SEO Communities**: Search terms like "workaround", "spreadsheet template", "tedious", "takes hours".
- **Product Hunt**: Monitor launches in your niche. Identify high-engagement launches (100+ upvotes).

## 3. The Lead Mining Playbook
When a competitor launch or trending post gets high engagement, execute the following mining process:
1. **Locate the Post**: Find high-engagement posts (100+ likes/comments) about tools in the SEO/marketing space.
2. **Export Engagers**: Scrape or list all users who commented "I need this", upvoted, or interacted with the post. These individuals have explicitly raised their hands acknowledging the problem.
3. **Save Profiles**: Compile names, handles (Twitter/LinkedIn), and emails if available into the opportunity template.
4. **Draft Warm Outreach Context**: Create a dedicated segment of leads tied directly to the source post context.

## 4. Reddit Engagement & Avoiding Bans
Reddit users have a high sensitivity to promotional spam and AI-generated noise. Follow these rules for engagement:
- **Drafting**: Write the first draft of your response using AI to lay down structured points.
- **Humanizing**: Run the response through a humanization check (e.g. AI Reddit Humanizer). Rewrite in a conversational, first-person, casual tone. Remove generic AI idioms, structured bullet lists with identical prefixes, and overly formal introductions.
- **The Value Ratio**: Provide 10 helpful, value-adding comments before ever mentioning your own tool. When mentioning your tool, position it as a free, open-source solution or an early-access experiment seeking feedback.
