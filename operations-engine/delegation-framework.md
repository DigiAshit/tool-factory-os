# Operations Engine - Delegation & Co-Founder Agreement Framework

## 1. Trigger
Executed by the **CEO** and **SOP Manager** when bringing a new developer co-founder onto a validated project.

## 2. Recruitment & Onboarding Process
1. **Find Great Builders**: Identify talented developers who are looking to ship products and are willing to take zero salary in exchange for project-specific equity splits.
2. **Draft the Agreement**: Prepare the standard TMaker Project Agreement. Do not spend money on separate company setups.

## 3. Co-Founder Agreement Template

### TMaker Project Partnership Agreement

**Parent Holding Company**: TMaker  
**Project Asset Name**: [Product Name]  
**Co-Founder (Distributor)**: [Founder Name] (Ownership: [Percentage]%)  
**Co-Founder (Builder)**: [Maker Name] (Ownership: [Percentage]%)  

1. **Central Finance**: Co-founders acknowledge that all revenues and expenses will flow through the central accounts of TMaker. No separate banking or legal entities will be formed for this product prior to the $100k MRR split gate.
2. **Compensation**: Both co-founders agree to receive **zero salary**. Compensation is paid strictly as a monthly profit distribution.
3. **Monthly Profit Share Formula**: Net cash profits for the product will be calculated on the last day of each calendar month as:
   $$\text{Net Profit} = \text{Product Revenue} - \text{Direct Hosting/API/Transaction Costs}$$
   TMaker will distribute the net profits to the co-founders' designated accounts within 5 business days of the month-end calculation.
4. **IP Assignment**: All code, designs, databases, customer lists, and intellectual property created for the product are assigned to TMaker.
5. **The $100k MRR Spin-off Gate**: In the event that the product reaches **$100,000 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** and sustains it for 2 consecutive months, either co-founder can request the creation of a separate legal entity. Upon formation, TMaker will transfer all product IP to the new entity.
6. **Exit Clause**: If either co-founder wishes to exit the project, they must provide 30 days' notice. The remaining co-founder retains a right of first refusal to purchase the exiting partner's profit-share interest at a valuation of $3\times$ the trailing 3-month average monthly profit.
